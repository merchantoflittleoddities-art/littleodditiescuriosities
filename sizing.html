Sizing Guide

Every treasure is handcrafted to sit comfortably on the wrist. Use this chart to find the perfect fit for your journey.

Bracelet Sizes

All measurements below are wrist circumference ranges in centimetres.

Size Wrist Circumference Intended For
Miniature Sprout 12–14 cm Children / very small wrists
Vines 14–16 cm Thinner / smaller wrists
Mushrooms 22–24 cm Medium / average wrists
Forest Floor 20–23 cm Slightly larger wrists
Wicked Branches 30–34 cm XL / larger wrists

All bracelets are currently made with sliding knots, sizes may vary

How to Measure

Follow these simple steps to measure your wrist in centimetres:

  1. Wrap a soft measuring tape around your wrist, where the bracelet will sit.
  2. If you do not have a measuring tape, wrap a piece of string or paper around your wrist, mark where it overlaps, then measure that length against a ruler.
  3. Measure in centimetres (cm).
  4. Measure comfortably, rather than pulling the tape tightly.

Between Sizes?

If your wrist falls between two sizes, choose the larger size for a little more room, or leave a note by sending word to the Merchant, or in the order notes.

Custom Sizing

Custom sizing can also be discussed — simply give the Merchant your wrist measurement in centimetres, and they can tailor a treasure just for you.
