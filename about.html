🌙 Welcome

Meet the Merchant

Welcome, traveller. Every curiosity shop has a story, and this one begins with a love of little treasures, forgotten wonders and the magic hidden within everyday things. I'm so glad you've wandered through my door.

🕯️ Why Little Oddities Curiosities?

Little Oddities Curiosities began with a love for whimsical treasures and the belief that you can never have enough little oddities tucked away in your cabinet. Every bracelet is handmade with care, created to become a tiny keepsake with a story all of its own.

🗝️ The Inspiration Behind the Name

I wanted every piece to feel like something you'd stumble across in a tiny old tavern or hidden curiosity shop. Somewhere filled with mysterious shelves, forgotten relics and curious little discoveries where you never quite know what you'll find next.

✨ Why Does Every Treasure Have a Story?

Every bracelet begins with an idea, a feeling or a spark of inspiration. Some are imagined from magical worlds, while others are inspired by hope, resilience or quiet moments that deserve remembering.

Giving every treasure its own story means each creation feels unique, waiting to find the keeper it was always meant for.

📿 Handmade with Care

Every treasure is carefully handmade rather than mass produced. Small variations are part of what makes each bracelet special, meaning no two treasures are ever completely identical.

💜 Thank You

Whether you've simply stopped by to browse or you're searching for the perfect treasure to take home, thank you for supporting a small independent creator. I hope you find something that feels as though it was waiting just for you.
